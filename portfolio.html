04 / How I work

Thoughtful, not complicated.

A flexible process that keeps the problem clear, the collaboration open, and the final experience grounded in real needs.

STEP 01

Understand

Clarify the audience, business goal, constraints, and the real problem to solve.

STEP 02

Explore

Map user journeys, sketch options, and build a structure before polishing visuals.

STEP 03

Design

Create accessible, responsive interfaces with clear hierarchy and reusable patterns.

STEP 04

Refine

Prototype, gather feedback, and collaborate on a practical developer handoff.
