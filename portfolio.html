Jalal Electronics — Product Marketing

A product-first campaign concept balancing clear hierarchy, product focus, and promotional messaging for retail audiences.

Case study overview

Product: Panasonic MK-5087M-NS Food Processor.

Offer shown in the campaign: Rs 32,900 sale price, reduced from Rs 44,900.

Design decisions: Keep the product as the visual hero, make the sale price the quickest detail to scan, and preserve a clear distinction between offer price and original price.

Scope note: These are campaign details and design rationale, not verified sales or conversion results.

06 / How I work

Thoughtful, not complicated.

A flexible process that keeps the problem clear, the collaboration open, and the final experience grounded in real needs.

STEP 01

Understand

Clarify the audience, business goal, constraints, and the real problem to solve.

STEP 02

Explore

Map user journeys, sketch options, and build a structure before polishing visuals.

STEP 03

Design

Create accessible, responsive interfaces with clear hierarchy and reusable patterns.

STEP 04

Refine

Prototype, gather feedback, and collaborate on a practical developer handoff.
